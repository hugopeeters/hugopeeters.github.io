A simulation of evolutionary steering behaviours. Green dots are food, red dots are poison. Offspring inherit traits from their parent, with random mutations. Statistics show the distribution of families (common ancestors), age and generations. The hightlighted vehicle is the current top performer and shows it's specific traits.
